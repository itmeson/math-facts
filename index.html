Facility blends how reliably you get a fact right on the first try with how quickly you answer it. Answers under about 1.5 s count as fully automatic; past about 6 s the fact is probably being computed rather than recalled. Getting it right on the second try earns partial credit — less than knowing it outright, more than not knowing it — and only unaided first-try answers are timed, since a retry measures re-calculation rather than recall. Only your last 6 encounters with each fact count, so old struggles fade as you improve. Facts you know least well come up more often. Results for facts outside the current range are kept — widen the range to see them again.
